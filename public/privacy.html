Privacy Policy

Taskflow ("we", "our", "us") operates the web application available at taskflow-ultimate.netlify.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Locally stored data: All your tasks, habits, goals, notes, mood logs, profiles, and app settings are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Firebase (optional collaboration): If you choose to use the real-time collaboration feature, selected task data may be synced via Google Firebase Firestore. This is entirely optional, clearly labelled, and requires your explicit action to enable. Firebase usage is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Voice input: If you use the microphone/voice input feature, audio is processed entirely by your browser's built-in Web Speech API. No audio data is recorded, stored, or transmitted by Taskflow.

Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and collect anonymised usage data to serve relevant ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and Advertising Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Taskflow productivity service
  • To display advertisements via Google AdSense that help keep Taskflow free
  • To enable optional real-time collaboration features via Firebase
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes

3. Cookies and Tracking

Taskflow itself uses localStorage (not cookies) for all app data and preferences. Google AdSense may set cookies to serve personalised advertisements based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised ads at adssettings.google.com or via your browser's cookie settings.

4. Data Security

Since all your task data is stored locally in your browser, it is protected by your device's own security. We recommend keeping your browser and device software up to date.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by going to Settings → Clear Data inside the app, or by clearing your browser's site data for taskflow-ultimate.netlify.app. Firebase collaboration data can be deleted by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Taskflow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our service, please contact us immediately.

7. Third-Party Services
